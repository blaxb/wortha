FAQ

Who is Wortha for?
Wortha is built for micro-creators (5K–100K followers) who are actively pitching brands or receiving sponsorship offers. If you've ever wondered "am I charging enough?" or "how do I counter this offer?" — this is for you. We're focused on independent creators who negotiate their own deals, not those with full-time managers.
Do I need an agency to use this?
No. Wortha is designed specifically for independent creators who don't have agents or managers. The AI negotiation assistant and benchmarks dashboard give you the same data-backed confidence that agencies provide their clients — without the 20-40% commission cut.
Is this only for one platform?
We support TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and other social platforms.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your billing portal.
Will my pricing data be shared?
Individual data stays private. Aggregated, anonymized data may be used to improve benchmarks.
